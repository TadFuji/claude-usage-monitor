# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent to each other. Assume the person on the other side is acting in good
faith, is short on time, and knows something you do not.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism about the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that "no" is a complete answer to a feature request on someone else's
  project, and that maintainers owe you nothing.
- Do not post other people's credentials, tokens, or personal data — including
  your own by accident. This project is about authentication tokens; be careful
  what you paste.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments; sexualised language or imagery;
publishing someone's private information; and any behaviour that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report anything you consider a problem to the maintainer at
<fujikawa@gmail.com>. Reports stay private. The maintainer may edit, delete, or
reject any contribution or comment, and may block anyone whose behaviour makes
this a worse place to be — with or without a warning, depending on how bad it
is.

This is a small project maintained by one person. There is no committee and no
appeals process; there is just an expectation that adults behave like adults.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
